Google Calendar subscription

Subscribe to this private URL in Google Calendar to see your property deadlines (sub-phases and target close dates) on your calendar. Google polls the feed every few hours, so changes will appear within that window.

How to subscribe
  1. Copy the URL above.
  2. Open Google Calendar.
  3. In the left sidebar, click the + next to "Other calendars" → From URL.
  4. Paste the URL → Add calendar.
  5. Your deadlines appear as a new calendar (named "Legacy Homes — [your name]"). You can rename or color it like any other calendar.
Regenerate URL

If you ever need to invalidate this URL (for example if you shared it accidentally), regenerate it. The old URL stops working immediately — you'll need to re-subscribe in Google Calendar with the new one.
